Patch, Teifi estuary

Checking wind and tide

Wind, rain, daylight, waves, swell and river flow from Open-Meteo. The wave figures are a forecast for the open bay off Cardigan Island. Solid green is inside your limits, hatched amber is marginal.

This is a planning aid. After high water the ebb at Patch runs out over the bar to open sea, so look at the water and the wind before you launch.
